01 Overview

The problem

General-knowledge learning can feel unstructured and disposable. SageSpire explores a coherent progression system that rewards consistency while keeping reliable sources at the center.

Goals

  • Turn broad learning into an understandable journey
  • Reward meaningful practice instead of empty activity
  • Keep generated guidance connected to reliable sources

My contribution

  • Developed the tower progression concept
  • Defined the learning and reward loops
  • Outlined content reliability requirements
  • Planned a future source-aware AI tutor
